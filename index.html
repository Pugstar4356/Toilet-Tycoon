♻️ Auto release

Pets that match any rule below are released the moment they hatch, and you get their release cash. A rule matches a pet when its rarity is ticked and its boost is ticked (no boosts ticked = any boost). Secret pets and locked pets are never auto-released.

🥚 Egg shop

Egg tier matches room tier

Rarity odds are set per egg tier (tap Odds on an egg to see them). Higher tier eggs give stronger pets. 🍀 Each egg tier has its own Luck upgrade that improves its rarity odds (secret pets are never affected by luck). 🌟 Secret pets are insanely rare from Tier 1 eggs and get easier to find with every egg tier. Pets survive prestige but are lost when you ascend.
